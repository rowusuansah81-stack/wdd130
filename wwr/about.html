Rapid Waters Rafting

Our purpose is simple: to get people off the couch and onto the river. We believe the best conversations happen with a paddle in your hands and cold water in your face. Our mission is to deliver safe, thrilling, and unforgettable white water adventures for families, friends, and first-timers alike.

History

Rapid Waters Rafting began in a leaky garage with two secondhand rafts and a very optimistic dog. What started as weekend trips for friends quickly grew into a full-time passion. Over the years we've guided thousands of guests down some of the most scenic stretches of river in the region, always keeping safety first and fun a very close second.

Today we run trips on five different rivers and have introduced more than a million guests to the thrill of white water. Our mission has never changed: safety first, unforgettable fun always.

Adventure Awaits You

From gentle half-day floats to heart-pounding Class IV rapids, there is a trip for every age and every level of courage. Grab a helmet, listen to your guide, and get ready for the ride of your life.
